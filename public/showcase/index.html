What this page is

A demonstration, and it is our own product on the stand.

The big product launches all use the same trick: a 3D object rendered to a sequence of still frames, played on a canvas by the scrollbar instead of by a clock. It looks like a live 3D engine. It is a flipbook — which is exactly why it stays smooth on a phone.

We built the object in Blender, lit it, rendered the turn, and wired it to the scroll. If you sell something you can photograph, we can do this for your page.

How this page was made

No 3D engine runs in your browser.

ObjectModelled and lit in Blender
RenderCycles, 120 frames, one full turn
DeliveryWebP with transparency, two sizes
PlaybackOne canvas, one drawImage per scroll frame
LibrariesNone
Sequence weight1.5 MB at 900px · 1.0 MB at 560px

The whole thing is one HTML file, one canvas and a folder of images. No framework, no scroll library, nothing to keep updated. That is the point: it has to still work in two years, when nobody is maintaining it.
